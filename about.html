Our Story

A Pittsburgh team.
A family-first
commitment.

Trinity Home Care was founded because we saw a need that wasn't being met — not just for skilled care, but for care that felt like it came from someone who truly cared. Too many families were being served by national franchises where care plans were templated and caregivers were interchangeable. We knew home care could be better.

We built Trinity in Pittsburgh because this is our home. We've seen firsthand how difficult it is to navigate care decisions for an aging parent — the guilt, the uncertainty, the fear of making the wrong choice for someone you love. We built this agency to be the answer we wish we'd had.

We're not a franchise. There's no corporate headquarters deciding how your loved one is cared for. When you call Trinity, you reach a real person who knows your family's situation and remembers your name the next time you call.

Our Caregivers

Every caregiver meets
a standard we'd hold
for our own family.

The person entering your loved one's home is the most important hiring decision we make. Our process is rigorous because it has to be.

  1. 01

    Personal Interview

    Every applicant meets face-to-face with our leadership team. We evaluate character, empathy, and commitment — not just credentials.

  2. 02

    Comprehensive Background Check

    A thorough criminal background screening at the national, state, and county level. No exceptions.

  3. 03

    Reference Verification

    We personally call prior employers and character references. If something doesn't check out, we don't move forward.

  4. 04

    Skills Assessment

    We evaluate each caregiver's practical abilities and match them to the specific needs of our clients — not just availability on a schedule.

  5. 05

    Thoughtful Matching

    When we introduce a caregiver to your family, we've already carefully considered compatibility, personality, and shared interests — not just scheduling.

Our caregivers are Trinity employees — fully bonded, insured, and supervised throughout their time with your family.
