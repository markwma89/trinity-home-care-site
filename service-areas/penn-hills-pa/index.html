Serving Penn Hills, PA

In-home care for Penn Hills families that truly makes a difference

Penn Hills is one of the Pittsburgh region's most populous and diverse communities — a place where families from many backgrounds have built lives together. For the seniors who have made Penn Hills their home, Trinity Home Care provides personalized, private pay in-home care that supports their independence, safety, and daily quality of life.

Trinity's caregivers understand that meaningful care starts with listening. We take the time to learn each client's story — their routines, their preferences, and what brings them comfort — and we build care around that understanding. From personal care support to meaningful companionship, Trinity is present for the moments that matter.

Trinity Home Care does not accept insurance claims. All services are private pay / direct pay only. Call us or request an assessment online — a free, no-obligation visit is the first step.
