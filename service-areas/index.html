Where We Serve

Serving Pittsburgh families close to home

Trinity Home Care is a locally owned, private pay home care agency based in Coraopolis, PA. We serve families throughout the Pittsburgh metro area — from the South Hills to the North Hills, from the western suburbs near the airport to the eastern communities along the Monongahela and Allegheny Rivers.

Whether your loved one is recovering from surgery, managing a chronic condition, or simply needs dependable daily support to remain safely at home, Trinity can help. Our caregivers are carefully selected and matched to each client — and we serve the neighborhoods where you live.

All Trinity Home Care services are private pay / direct pay only. We do not accept insurance claims. This allows us to maintain flexible, personalized care arrangements without the constraints of insurance authorization. Our care coordinators are happy to walk you through service options and pricing during a free assessment.

What We Offer

In-home care services available throughout the Pittsburgh area

Wherever you are in the Pittsburgh region, Trinity Home Care brings the same high standard of care. Our services include:

  • Personal Care — bathing, grooming, dressing, and hygiene support
  • Companion Care — conversation, activities, and social engagement
  • Medication Reminders — consistent, reliable prompts for safe daily routines
  • Meal Preparation — home-cooked meals tailored to dietary needs
  • Light Housekeeping — a clean, safe, organized home environment
  • Mobility & Safety Support — fall prevention and safe movement assistance
  • Dementia & Alzheimer's Care — specialized support for cognitive decline
  • Respite Care — relief for family caregivers
  • Post-Hospital Recovery — support during critical transitions home
  • Veteran Care — respectful, tailored care for those who served

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Trinity Home Care serves families throughout the greater Pittsburgh metro area, including communities across Allegheny County such as Mt. Lebanon, Bethel Park, Upper St. Clair, Monroeville, Penn Hills, Ross Township, McCandless, Moon Township, Robinson Township, South Fayette, Shaler Township, Plum, West Mifflin, Fox Chapel, and Sewickley — along with many surrounding neighborhoods. If you're unsure whether we serve your specific address, contact us and we'll let you know.

Trinity Home Care does not accept insurance claims. All services are private pay / direct pay only. This means payment is made directly by the client or family, without insurance authorization. Our care coordinator can walk you through service options and what to expect during a free assessment.

The first step is a free in-home assessment — no obligation. Call us at 412-345-3721 or submit a request through our contact page. We'll schedule a time to visit, learn about your loved one's needs, and explain what care options are available in your community.

We understand that home care needs can arise suddenly. We typically respond within one business hour and can often arrange care to begin quickly after the initial assessment. If you have an urgent situation, please call us directly at 412-345-3721.
