Serving Mt. Lebanon, PA

Reliable in-home care for Mt. Lebanon families

Mt. Lebanon is one of the Pittsburgh region's most established and close-knit communities — and families here hold high standards for the care their loved ones receive. Trinity Home Care shares those values. As a locally owned, private pay home care agency based in Coraopolis, we bring thoughtful, personalized in-home support to seniors and adults throughout Mt. Lebanon and the South Hills.

Whether your parent needs daily assistance with personal care and morning routines, or your spouse is recovering from a recent health event and needs attentive transitional support, Trinity's caregivers are matched carefully to each client. We learn your loved one's preferences, daily rhythms, and what matters most — and we build care around that reality.

Trinity Home Care does not accept insurance claims. All services are private pay / direct pay only. Our care coordinator is happy to explain what that means for your family during a free, no-obligation in-home assessment.
