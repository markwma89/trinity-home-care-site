Serving Ross Township, PA

In-home care that north Pittsburgh families can depend on

Ross Township is one of the North Pittsburgh area's most established residential communities — a place where families have set down roots for generations. For seniors who call Ross Township home and want to stay there, Trinity Home Care provides the personalized, private pay in-home support that makes independent living possible and sustainable.

Trinity's caregivers are matched to clients with care — considering not just the practical needs, but the personality and preferences that make each person unique. Whether your loved one needs daily personal care assistance, companionship visits to break the isolation of living alone, or dedicated recovery support after a health event, Trinity delivers with consistency and genuine warmth.

Trinity Home Care does not accept insurance claims. All services are private pay / direct pay only. Call us to schedule a free, no-obligation in-home assessment — we'll answer every question your family has.
