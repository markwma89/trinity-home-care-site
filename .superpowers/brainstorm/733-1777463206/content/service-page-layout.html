A — Focused (4 sections)

Hero · Description & benefits · Who it helps · CTA. Clean and fast to build. Works well for services with a narrow audience.

Pros

  • Fastest to build
  • Low risk of thin content
  • Stays on-topic

Cons

  • Less SEO depth
  • Fewer internal links
★ Hero — name + photo
§ What it is + benefits
§ Who it helps

§ 2–3 service FAQs

B — Standard (6 sections)

Adds a "Signs you might need this" section (great for search intent like "when does someone need companion care") and 2–3 FAQs specific to that service.

Pros

  • Solid SEO depth
  • Answers real questions
  • Matches search intent

Cons

  • Needs good FAQ content
★ Hero — name + photo

C — Rich (9 sections)

Adds a care scenario narrative, a service-specific testimonial, and a "Related services" strip linking to other service pages. Maximum content depth and internal linking.

Pros

  • Best SEO potential
  • Strong internal linking
  • Most persuasive

Cons

  • Most content to write
  • More complex build
