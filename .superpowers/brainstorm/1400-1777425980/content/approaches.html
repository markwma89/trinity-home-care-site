A — Vertical Journey Recommended

All 5 steps stacked vertically on the left with a connected numbered flow, one contextual photo column on the right that stays as you scroll. Matches the homepage 3-step pattern but richer. White background with ivory alternates below.

  • Familiar — evolves existing pattern
  • Mobile-first naturally
  • Easiest to build
  • Focused, linear reading
  • Photo stays static (less per-step context)
  • Less visual drama per step

B — Alternating Image Sections

Each of the 5 steps is its own full section with a dedicated photo. Image and copy alternate sides (left/right) and backgrounds toggle white ↔ ivory. Step 5 uses navy for gravitas. Very immersive — feels like a micro-story.

  • Each step gets a real photo
  • Most immersive storytelling
  • Strong visual rhythm
  • Long page — heavy scroll
  • 5 unique photos needed
  • More CSS to maintain

C — Timeline + Expandable Cards

A horizontal gold-to-teal progress line with 5 numbered milestone dots across the top, then 5 compact cards below. Clicking a card expands its full detail section below the timeline. Unique, modern — but more JS complexity.

  • Most visually distinctive
  • Compact — no long scroll
  • Interactive engagement
  • Requires JS for expand
  • Mobile timeline is tricky
  • Risks hiding content
