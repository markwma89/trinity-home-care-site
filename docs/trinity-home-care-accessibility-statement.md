# Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** April 22, 2026

Trinity Home Care is committed to making its website accessible and usable for all visitors, including people with disabilities. We believe every individual should be able to access information about our services with dignity, ease, and confidence.

## Our Commitment

We are working to improve the accessibility and usability of the Trinity Home Care website so that it is more inclusive for all users, regardless of ability, device, or assistive technology.

Our goal is to provide a website experience that is clear, accessible, and respectful of the needs of all visitors.

## Accessibility Efforts

Trinity Home Care takes reasonable steps to support website accessibility, including efforts such as:

- using clear page structure and heading hierarchy
- providing meaningful alternative text for important images where appropriate
- supporting keyboard navigation where possible
- maintaining readable color contrast and legible typography
- designing pages to be responsive across devices and screen sizes
- using consistent navigation and interface patterns
- reviewing and improving accessibility features as the site evolves

## Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We recognize that some areas of the website may still be in the process of improvement, and we are committed to reviewing accessibility barriers and making reasonable updates over time.

As website content, functionality, and design evolve, we aim to continue improving accessibility wherever practical.

## Third-Party Content

Some portions of the website may include third-party content, embedded tools, maps, videos, or services that are not fully controlled by Trinity Home Care. While we aim to choose tools and services thoughtfully, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content or platforms.

## Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the Trinity Home Care website, encounter an accessibility barrier, or need assistance with any content, we encourage you to contact us.

We welcome feedback and will make reasonable efforts to address accessibility concerns and provide the information you need in an alternative format when appropriate.

## Accessibility Requests

When contacting us about an accessibility issue, please include, if possible:

- the page or feature you were trying to access
- a brief description of the issue
- the device or browser you were using
- any assistive technology you were using, if applicable

This information can help us better understand the issue and respond more effectively.

## Policy Updates

Trinity Home Care may update this Accessibility Statement from time to time as our website and accessibility efforts continue to evolve. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
