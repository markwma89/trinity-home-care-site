# Home Care Competitor Scorecard
## Trinity Home Care — 30-Site Analysis

Scores are 1–10 in each category. Final ranking ordered strongest to weakest.

**Scoring Categories:**
- **Visual Design** — Quality, modernity, cohesion, photography, typography
- **UX Clarity** — Navigation, information hierarchy, ease of finding what you need
- **Trust Building** — Social proof, certifications, review scores, caregiver credibility, longevity
- **Conversion Strategy** — CTA strength, intake flow, phone number prominence, form design
- **Content Structure** — Services clarity, page organization, educational depth
- **Mobile Quality** — Responsive design, mobile UX, thumb-friendly navigation
- **Overall Effectiveness** — Does it feel like a site you'd trust with your parent's care?

## Top 5 Best Service Page Structures

1. **TheKey** — Services organized by condition (Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, Heart Health, Cancer, Stroke, Hospital to Home, End-of-Life) — highly specific and searchable
2. **BAYADA** — Services organized by age group (Seniors 65+, Adults 19-64, Children newborn-18) — intuitive self-selection
3. **Homewatch CareGivers** — Total Care Solutions™ tiered model (Active, Wellness, Personal, Transition, Care on Demand, Specialized) — mirrors care journey progression
4. **Home Instead** — Clear card grid with icons, human descriptions, links to detailed service pages; includes Enhanced Care Services for nurse-directed tasks
5. **Senior Helpers** — Condition-specific services (Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, Chronic Disease, Surgery Assistance, VA Benefits) + interactive quiz

## Top 5 Best Contact / Conversion Flows

1. **Home Instead** — Hero intake wizard (who needs care?) → routes to local office finder or direct phone
2. **Senior Helpers** — "Schedule a LIFE Profile Assessment" CTA → specific, meaningful, differentiates from generic "Contact Us"
3. **Comfort Keepers** — Self-assessment quiz → in-home assessment → real-time care plan — transparent 3-step process
4. **Homewatch CareGivers** — "Get Care Today" → contact form with service interest checkboxes + local office finder
5. **TheKey** — "Get Started" → "Your Care Needs" assessment flow → personalized service matching
