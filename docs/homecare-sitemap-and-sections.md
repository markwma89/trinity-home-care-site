## Core Pages — Detailed Section Breakdown

---

### HOME PAGE (/)

**Goal:** Convert an anxious adult child into a care inquiry within one session.

| Section | Content | Static or Shared |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Hero** | Headline, sub-heading, primary CTA ("Get a Free Care Assessment"), secondary CTA ("See Our Services"), phone number, Google rating with review count | Static |
| **Trust Strip** | 3–4 stat callouts: clients served, Google rating, years of service, caregiver screening standard | Static |
| **Services Overview** | 6–8 service cards (icon, title, 1-sentence description, "Learn more" link) | Shared (pulls from Services pages) |
| **Why Trinity Section** | Proprietary approach/methodology, 4–5 differentiators, owner/brand message | Static |
| **Testimonials** | 5+ testimonials carousel: name, city, relationship, star rating, specific quote | Shared (pulls from Reviews) |
| **How It Works** | 4-step process: Assessment → Matching → Care Begins → Ongoing Support | Static |
| **Family Reassurance** | Emotional copy addressing adult child anxiety; warm caregiver-client photo | Static |
| **Certifications Strip** | Logo strip: HCAOA, Alzheimer's Association, Home Care Pulse, BBB, State License | Shared (pulls from About/Certifications) |
| **Caregiver Profiles** | 2–3 named caregivers with photo, name, tenure, quote | Shared (pulls from About/Caregivers) |
| **Careers CTA** | Brief invite for caregivers; "Apply Now" CTA | Shared |
| **Final CTA / Contact** | Form (name, phone, zip, who needs care) + contact info + owner photo | Static |
| **FAQ Preview** | 3–4 top FAQs collapsed, with "See All FAQs" link | Shared (pulls from FAQ) |

### INDIVIDUAL SERVICE PAGES (/services/[service-name])

**Each service page follows the same template structure:**

| Section | Content | Static or Shared |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Page Hero** | Service-specific headline + caregiver photo relevant to service | Static per page |
| **What Is [Service]?** | 2–3 paragraph explanation; what it includes; who it's for | Static per page |
| **What's Included** | Bulleted task list specific to this service | Static per page |
| **Signs You May Need [Service]** | 4–6 observable signs or situations triggering this care need | Static per page |
| **Who Provides Care?** | Brief on caregiver training for this specific service type | Static per page |
| **Testimonial (specific)** | 1 testimonial from a client/family who used this exact service | Shared |
| **Related Services** | 3 "you might also need" service cards | Shared |
| **CTA** | "Start [Service Name] Care Today" — phone + form | Shared |

**Priority service pages (build first):**
1. Companion Care (highest search volume, simplest to understand)
2. Personal Care (second highest, most common need)
3. Dementia & Alzheimer's Care (highest anxiety, most searched by family caregivers)
4. Respite Care (underserved by competitors; high emotional need)
5. 24-Hour / Live-In Care (highest-value clients)

### FOR FAMILIES (/for-families)

**Goal:** Build trust through education during the research phase; convert readers into contacts.

#### What Is Home Care? (/for-families/what-is-home-care)
- Definition: non-medical vs. skilled/medical home care
- What a typical care day looks like
- What caregivers can and cannot do
- How home care differs from assisted living
- Who benefits most from in-home care
- Cost overview (link to cost page)
- Video if available (even a simple explainer)

#### How Much Does Home Care Cost? (/for-families/cost-of-home-care)
- Hourly rate range for the region (transparent)
- Live-in vs. hourly vs. 24-hour rate structures
- What affects cost (level of care, hours per week, specialization)
- How to pay: private pay, long-term care insurance, VA benefits, Medicaid waiver (if applicable)
- "How much care is enough?" guidance
- CTA: "Get a personalized cost estimate" → contact

#### Home Care vs. Assisted Living (/for-families/home-care-vs-assisted-living)
- Comparison table: cost, flexibility, personalization, social interaction, independence
- Emotional argument for aging in place
- When assisted living becomes necessary
- How Trinity helps families navigate this decision

#### Family Caregiver Resources (/for-families/caregiver-resources)
- Guides and articles for adult children managing a parent's care
- Links to blog posts on topics like: "How to talk to your parent about accepting help," "Signs your parent needs more care," "Avoiding caregiver burnout"
- External resources: local Area Agency on Aging, Alzheimer's Association

### BLOG (/blog)

**Goal:** Drive organic search traffic on family caregiver queries; establish Trinity as a trusted resource.

**Content Strategy — Priority Topics:**
1. Signs your parent needs home care (very high search volume)
2. How to talk to a parent about accepting help
3. What to look for in a home care agency
4. Home care vs. assisted living cost comparison
5. How to pay for home care (LTC insurance, VA benefits, Medicaid)
6. Caregiver burnout: what it is and how to prevent it
7. Dementia care at home: what families need to know
8. What does a home care caregiver actually do?
9. How to prepare your home for a caregiver
10. What questions to ask when hiring a home care agency

**Publishing cadence:** 2 posts per month minimum. Quality > quantity. Each post 800–1,500 words with one strong CTA embedded.

### FAQ (/faq)

**Goal:** Answer the 10–15 most common questions to reduce barriers to contact.

**Content:**
Organized in categories:

**Getting Started:**
- How do I know if my parent needs home care?
- What happens when I call Trinity?
- How quickly can care start?
- Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

**About Our Caregivers:**
- How do you select caregivers?
- What if I don't get along with my caregiver?
- What happens if my caregiver is sick?
- Are your caregivers employees or independent contractors?

**Services & Care:**
- What can a home caregiver do? What can't they do?
- Can I customize the care schedule?
- Do you provide care overnight or on weekends?
- Can you help with medications?

**Cost & Payment:**
- How much does home care cost?
- Is home care covered by Medicare?
- Do you accept long-term care insurance?
- Can veterans get help paying for home care?

## Static vs. Shared/Reusable Content

| Content Type | Approach |
|--------------|----------|
| Hero sections (per page) | Static — unique to each page |
| Trust Strip | Shared component — used on Home, Services, How It Works |
| Testimonials | Shared — pulled from a central testimonials database; filtered by service or use case per page |
| Caregiver Profiles | Shared — central database; featured on Home, About, Careers |
| Certification Logos | Shared — displayed on Home, About, Services |
| Final CTA / Contact Form | Shared component — appears at bottom of every core page |
| FAQ items | Shared — FAQ page contains all; homepage/How It Works show a curated subset |
| Blog posts | Standalone — linked from For Families, related service pages |
| Service Cards | Shared — used on Home Services Overview, and Services page |
| Navigation | Shared — consistent across all pages |
| Footer | Shared — consistent across all pages; includes contact info, services nav, certifications, social |
