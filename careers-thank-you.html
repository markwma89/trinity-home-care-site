What to expect

Here’s What Happens Next

  1. 1

    We Review Your Application

    Every application is reviewed personally by a member of our team — never filtered by an automated system. We take our time to read what you’ve shared.

  2. 2

    We Reach Out Within Two Business Days

    If it seems like there’s a potential fit, someone from Trinity will contact you — usually by phone — to introduce ourselves and ask a few questions.

  3. 3

    We Have a Real Conversation

    We want to make sure Trinity is the right fit for you, just as much as we want to make sure you’re the right fit for us. No pressure — just an honest conversation about the opportunity.
